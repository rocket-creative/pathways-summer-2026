## What is acupuncture and how does it work?

Acupuncture is designed to stimulate your body’s internal systems by engaging them through careful and precise insertion of very fine needles into key trigger points. Using carefully honed knowledge of those specific trigger points across the body, acupuncture needles are inserted gently into the skin by a certified acupuncturist. The insertion of the needle is nearly painless, only enough sensation to disrupt the nerve signals and cue your body’s response.

The acupuncture needles are left in long enough to engage your relevant body systems that jumpstarts rejuvenation, healing, and relaxation in a calm and comfortable environment. When performed by a certified reflexologist or acupuncturist, acupuncture is a safe and beneficial alternative or complement to other treatments for a variety of concerns.

## Acupuncture as a science and ancient art

Though it’s recognized by modern medicine as a valuable part of healing and treating many conditions, acupuncture dates back much further. It’s rooted in traditional Chinese medicine and has been used for thousands of years to help alleviate pain and restore the balance of the body’s natural energies. These traditional roots translate into modern medicine as well. Acupuncture has now been recognized by the World Health Organization as a viable treatment for a number of conditions and symptoms.

The ancient use of acupuncture focuses on the harmony of life energies (qi) by chanelling them through established pathways (called meridians) in your body. Acupuncture helps guide and direct the qi back to its ideal location through the insertion of the thin needles into key areas to re-balance your body and energies. This was thought (and is now proven ) to help stimulate a more receptive and responsive nervous system balance while reducing pain.
