## Domino

With his calm presence, gentle eyes, and intuitive heart, Domino brings comfort and connection to every client he meets. Known for offering the best snuggles and silent support, he instinctively knows when someone needs a little extra love. Whether he’s resting quietly during a session or offering a paw in solidarity, Domino reminds us all of the healing power of presence, patience, and unconditional affection. He’s truly a good boy—and an even better therapist.


## Gypsy Sassafras

Gypsy Sassafras was born on a dairy farm in upstate New York on August 22, 2022. Gypsy now lives in a beach community outside of NYC where she enjoys walks on the boardwalk, retrieving balls and running alongside her mom roller skating. You can see her at times popping up during virtual therapy sessions and when in person she enjoys a belly rub and greets clients with her happy dance. Gypsy is a kind soul with a warm smile who connects with people on an emotional level. She holds space for laughter and crying and comforts the most sensitive feelings.
